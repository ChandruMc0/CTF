sha-256 of the ledger below, lowercase hex. one newline after each line,
including the last. don't reorder or reformat it.

pentimento/witness/v1
origin=<x>,<y>
completed_utc=<timestamp>
record=<id>
root=<id>
snapshot_unix=<n>

put each value where its placeholder is, angle brackets removed, nothing else
on the line:

  <x>,<y>      decimal integers, one comma, no space, no sign.
  <timestamp>  iso-8601 utc to the whole minute: yyyy-mm-ddthh:mm:00z.
  <id>         the bare identifier as its own source writes it, no url and no
               prefix. record and root are not the same identifier.
  <n>          whole seconds since the unix epoch, for what lead.png was cut
               from.

the flag is that digest wrapped in zdk{}.
